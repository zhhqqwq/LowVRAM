# Code of Conduct

LowVRAM contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, review ideas on their technical merits, and make the project welcoming to people of different experience levels and backgrounds. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, or deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Reports should be sent privately to the project maintainers rather than posted publicly when they contain sensitive information.
